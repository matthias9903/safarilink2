    Acceptance. By accessing or using the SafariLink platform, the Supplier acknowledges that it has read, understood, and agreed to be bound by these Supplier Terms & Conditions. If the Supplier does not agree, it should not use the Platform or its services.

    1
    Purpose of SafariLink

    SafariLink is a B2B travel operating network that connects tour Operators with tourism Suppliers in Tanzania. The Platform supports discovery, booking communication, and management of accommodation and fleet services between SafariLink verified users (Operators) and Suppliers (Accommodation and Fleet Providers).

    2
    Supplier Eligibility

    To appear on SafariLink, a Supplier must be legally registered and licensed to operate its accommodation, camp, lodge, vehicle, or related services in Tanzania, and able to commit to fulfilling its listed services. The Supplier is responsible for ensuring it meets all legal, regulatory, and licensing requirements applicable to its operations and employees.

    3
    Supplier Verification

    SafariLink may request and verify business information and documentation, including registration, licenses, insurance, and premises details required to confirm a Supplier profile. The Supplier agrees to cooperate with verification requests and to promptly provide any updated or additional information when reasonably requested.

    4
    Accuracy of Supplier Information

    Suppliers must maintain accurate, current, and complete information on the platform, including descriptions, rates, availability, facilities, and contact details. SafariLink may request corrections where information appears materially inaccurate or outdated, and the Supplier must update its information without undue delay.

    5
    Accommodation Inventory

    Accommodation Suppliers must maintain accurate inventory information for their rooms, camps, lodges, or other accommodation units, providing current availability, rates, and property details so that Operators can plan Safari itineraries with reliable knowledge of the units available on the requested dates.

    6
    Fleet Inventory

    Fleet Suppliers must maintain accurate inventory information for their vehicles, including vehicle type, seating capacity, condition, equipment, and availability schedules. The Supplier ensures that such inventory is truthful and that fleet listings are active only for vehicles legitimately available in its fleet.

    7
    Private STO Rates

    SafariLink Tour Operator (STO) Rates may be agreed between the Operator and the Supplier. They are private lines, negotiated terms accessible only to Reservation agents on the SafariLink or Operators. Suppliers must not publish, share, or distribute these rates outside the agreed confidential channel.

    8
    Rate Validity

    Reported rates describe the period of validity during which they may be quoted. Suppliers should ensure rates reflect their applicable seasons and validity periods, and communicated changes according to the operation, as set out in the commercial agreement between the parties.

    9
    Rate Parity and Rate Accuracy

    Rates displayed to Operators should be accurate by providing complete rate information, with any mandatory supplements or conditions disclosed. Where comparisons available in the market, the Supplier should avoid structural misrepresentation of its own published rates so that Operators can plan effectively.

    10
    Availability

    Suppliers are responsible for maintaining accurate availability of the accommodation, rooms, and vehicles. Availability is acknowledged as the basis on which Operators rely on when planning itineraries and managing their client experience.

    11
    Reservation Requests

    Operators send Reservation Requests against availability displayed on the platform. Suppliers must respond to these requests in good faith, with accurate information about their capacity, rates, and pricing policies for the requested dates and traveler requirements.

    12
    Confirmed Reservations

    Once a Reservation is confirmed, the Supplier is obligated to honor it in accordance with the agreed terms, rates, and itinerary details. Any material change, including changes to service levels or pricing previously confirmed, must be communicated before the effective date and, where necessary, consented to.

    13
    Overbooking

    Suppliers should use reasonable efforts to avoid overbooking and to protect confirmed Reservations. Where permitted, overbooking should be managed without avoidable disruption, with prompt re-aterming or re-accommodation agreed and communicated with the Operator.

    14
    Supplier Response Times

    Suppliers are expected to respond to Reservation requests and operational inquiries within a reasonable defined window so that Operators can maintain itinerary accuracy and respond to their clients in a timely manner. Repeated failure to respond when applicable may affect the Supplier's search visibility.

    15
    Supplier Reliability

    Suppliers are responsible for fulfilling Confirmed Reservations in the standard expected of professional tourism businesses, including providing the accommodation, vehicles, and run services consistent with their marketing profile and the operator's reasonable expectations.

    16
    Cancellation by Supplier

    Where a Supplier must cancel a confirmed Reservation, it must promptly notify the Operator and use all reasonable efforts to provide a suitable alternative or re-accommodation of an equal or better standard at no additional, where required by the commercial agreement or applicable law.

    17
    Supplier Cancellation Policies

    Suppliers may maintain their public cancellation policies, provided the policies are accurately stated on the platform and applied consistently. Where a platform standard applies, the applicable policy published to the Supplier's profile governs.

    18
    Payments

    Payments are settled between Operators and the Supplier in accordance with the applicable commercial agreement and the platform's settlement processes. The terms of collection, invoicing, and payment timing are as described in the commercial or payment agreement established by the parties.

    19
    SafariLink Fees and Commission

    Where SafariLink charges fees or commissions the relevant fee structure is described in the applicable commercial agreement. Fees are applied at the point at which the parties agree, and the Supplier remains his pay obligations described in those agreements.

    20
    Taxes

    Suppliers are responsible for the correct declaration of taxes and duties applicable to their services and rates, including any VAT or similar levies. Where applicable, taxes should be provided transparently to the Operator so that the invoicing is accurate and compliant.

    21
    Accommodation Quality

    Accommodation Suppliers must offer accommodation, facilities, and services that satisfy health, safety, and quality standards applicable to their license in Tanzania, and that are at the standard represented on the platform, including when the accommodation is performed.

    22
    Vehicle Safety and Condition

    Fleet Suppliers must provide roadworthy, safe vehicles that are maintained in a proper and appropriate condition, with all necessary valid licensing, insurance, and safety equipment required by Tanzanian law, and described accurately on the platform.

    23
    Drivers

    Where drivers are provided with fleet services, they must be qualified, licensed, experienced, and fit to operate the assigned vehicle, and must comply with applicable road safety rules and the operator's reasonable instructions where communicated.

    24
    Breakdowns and Operational Incidents

    The Supplier must notify the Operator of any breakdown, incident, or interruption affecting a confirmed service as soon as reasonably possible, and must act in makeup efforts to provide alternatives, replacements, or advisory guidance consistent with operational standards and applicable law.

    25
    Supplier Communication

    The Supplier must maintain a single, reachable point of contact and respond to legitimate safariLink and Operator communication through the platform channels. All communications concerning Reservations and rates should be maintained so there is a proper record for operational and reporting purposes.

    26
    Documents

    Suppliers must maintain and, where requested, submit the legal, license-related and operational documents required for platform verification, including business registration, operating licenses, driver documentation and vehicle documentation, and must keep such records current.

    27
    Supplier Confidential Information

    SafariLink may receive confidential Supplier information including:

    • negotiated rates;
    • STO Rates;
    • supplier contracts;
    • commission structures;
    • inventory;
    • internal policies;
    • financial information;
    • business contacts; and
    • operational data.

    SafariLink will use such information only for legitimate Platform, contractual, operational, analytical, security, or legal purposes, subject to applicable agreements and law.

    28
    Data Protection

    Suppliers must comply with applicable data-protection and privacy requirements when submitting or receiving personal information through SafariLink.

    Suppliers must not upload unnecessary personal information.

    Where a Supplier provides traveler information to SafariLink, the Supplier represents that it has a lawful basis and appropriate authority to provide that information.

    Where necessary, SafariLink and the Supplier may enter into a separate Data Processing Agreement.

    29

    SafariLink may use AI to assist with:

    • Supplier matching;
    • search;
    • rate-sheet extraction;
    • inventory organization;
    • itinerary creation;
    • reservation assistance;
    • operational recommendations;
    • document processing;
    • analytics; and
    • communication drafting.

    AI-generated information does not replace Supplier confirmation. A Supplier's actual inventory, availability, rate, and reservation status remain the authoritative commercial information.

    Suppliers must review and correct information where necessary. SafariLink will not intentionally use AI to create false availability or rates.

The Supplier confirms that it has the necessary rights to provide the content.

    Suspension

    • information is materially inaccurate;
    • required documentation has expired;
    • repeated cancellations occur;
    • availability is consistently inaccurate;
    • payment obligations are overdue;
    • fraudulent activity is suspected;
    • security risks exist;
    • the Supplier breaches these Terms; or

    Termination

    Either party may terminate the Supplier relationship in accordance with the applicable commercial agreement.

    • material breach;
