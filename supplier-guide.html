Onboarding & Operational Workflow

1

Apply

Provide your core business information to begin the onboarding process.

Required: Registration details, business licenses, contact person, website, and operating locations.
2

Verification

SafariLink reviews your application to ensure platform quality and security. This may include verifying business identity, insurance, and professional qualifications.

3

Build Your Profile

Accurate profiles improve conversion. Depending on your sector, you will provide descriptions, facilities, vehicle specs, or guide experience.

4

Manage Rates & Availability

Upload your STO (trade) rates and maintain accurate availability. High-accuracy availability reduces failed requests and operational friction.

5

Respond & Confirm

Receive requests from operators. The platform distinguishes between Requested and Confirmed statuses—a critical distinction for operational safety.

Supplier Performance & Best Practices

To maintain a high-quality network, SafariLink monitors key reliability indicators:

✓ Response Time
✓ Confirmation Rate
✓ Availability Accuracy
✓ Operator Feedback

Best Practice: Communicate early. If an operational issue occurs with a confirmed booking, inform the operator immediately through the platform.
