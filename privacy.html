This Privacy Policy explains how SafariLink Tanzania B2B ("SafariLink," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, stores and discloses personal information when you use the SafariLink platform, website and services (together, the "Platform") and when you otherwise interact with us.

SafariLink operates as a B2B travel-operating network that connects tour operators, accommodation, fleet providers and professional guides across Tanzania. The Platform processes information about businesses, their authorized users, and — to a limited extent — travellers whose information is submitted by operator accounts.

By using the Platform, you acknowledge that your information will be handled as described in this Privacy Policy.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

SafariLink may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, the Platform, legal requirements, or for other operational reasons.

When we make material changes, we will notify users through the Platform, by email where appropriate, or through other reasonable means before the changes take effect.

The updated Privacy Policy will indicate the "Last Updated" date at the top of the page.

Your continued use of the Platform after the effective date of an updated Privacy Policy constitutes acceptance of those changes, to the extent permitted by applicable law. If you do not agree with the updated policy, you should discontinue use of the Platform and may close your Account.

Children's Privacy

The SafariLink Platform is designed for businesses and professional users. It is not directed at children under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Where an operator submits information about travellers that includes minors (such as for family safaris), the operator is responsible for obtaining appropriate consent from the parent or legal guardian.

If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child without appropriate consent, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information.
