Reservation Management

Keep every supplier booking clear from request to confirmation.

SafariLink gives both operators and providers one shared operational view of availability requests, confirmations, deadlines and booking information.

Two sides of reservation control

How requests become confirmed services

Tour operator

Turns the chosen services in the itinerary into trackable booking activity.

  • Sends availability and reservation requests with the relevant trip details.
  • Tracks responses, booking references, confirmation status and deadlines.
  • Maintains internal notes, task assignments, documents and client information.
  • Uses confirmed bookings to prepare vouchers and operate the trip confidently.

Supplier or service provider

Receives a clear request and responds in a structured way.

  • Reviews requested dates, service requirements and operator information.
  • Confirms availability, declines it or suggests a suitable alternative.
  • Shares booking reference, relevant conditions and operational information.
  • Sees confirmed work clearly, allowing the team to prepare for delivery.
