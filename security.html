Security

Security at SafariLink

Tourism operations depend on trust. SafariLink handles business information, supplier commercial information, client information, reservations, rates and operational data. Security is therefore not an optional feature — it is part of the platform foundation.

Security is shared between SafariLink and you. This page describes the security principles, controls and practices that underpin the platform. Users remain responsible for account security, credential protection, endpoint security and secure handling of their own access.

STO Rate Protection

Private B2B rates are commercially sensitive

🔒

STO rates are protected by layered access controls

SafariLink restricts access to private B2B/STO rates based on verified company status, user role, supplier permissions, commercial agreements and platform access rules. STO rates are not publicly searchable or visible to unauthorized users. Users must also comply with supplier confidentiality requirements — disclosing or redistributing private rates without authorization is a breach of platform terms.

Incident Response

If a security incident occurs

SafariLink maintains a defined incident-response process.

1

Detect

Identify the incident through monitoring, alerts or reports.

2

Contain

Isolate affected systems and prevent further impact.

3

Investigate

Determine scope, cause and affected information.

4

Remediate

Address root cause and restore secure operations.

5

Recover

Restore services and verify normal operation.

6

Communicate

Notify affected users and authorities where required.

Incident handling is risk-based. Where applicable law requires notification of a personal-data breach, SafariLink will make the required notifications within the applicable timeframe. Incident-response procedures are reviewed and tested periodically.
AI Security

AI does not override permissions

That is the core rule. SafariLink AI features operate within each user's existing access controls.

1

Permission-bound AI

If a user cannot access a supplier's private rate through the normal platform, the AI assistant will not expose that information either. AI operates within the user's existing access permissions.

2

Authorized data only

AI uses platform data that the user is authorized to access — it does not pull from sources the user cannot already see.

3

Respects access controls

Role-based restrictions, STO-rate protections and supplier permissions are applied before AI results are returned.

4

No invented operational data

AI avoids fabricating rates, availability, confirmations or operational information that does not exist in the platform.

5

Suggestions are not actions

AI clearly distinguishes suggestions and drafts from confirmed actions. Users must review and approve important decisions.

6

Confidentiality protected

AI responses are designed to protect confidential business information, STO rates and personal data.
