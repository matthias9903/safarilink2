SafariLink Tanzania B2B Operator Terms & Conditions

These Tour Operator Terms & Conditions ("Operator Terms") govern access to and use of SafariLink Tanzania B2B ("SafariLink", "we", "us", or "our") by tour operators, travel agencies, destination management companies, and other authorized tourism businesses ("Operator", "you", or "your").

SafariLink is a business-to-business tourism technology platform designed to connect tourism businesses with accommodation providers, vehicle and fleet providers, professional guides, transfer companies, activity providers, and other tourism suppliers.

The Platform enables Operators to discover suppliers, access eligible Special Tour Operator (STO) rates, check availability, request and manage reservations, lease vehicles, source guides, build itineraries, prepare quotations, and coordinate tourism operations.
