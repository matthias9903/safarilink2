These Tour Operator Terms & Conditions ("Operator Terms") govern access to and use of SafariLink Tanzania B2B ("SafariLink", "we", "us", or "our") by tour operators, travel agencies, destination management companies, and other authorized tourism businesses ("Operator", "you", or "your").

Applicable to Tour Operators
Contents

    By registering for an Operator account or using the Platform's Operator services, you agree to these Operator Terms.

    1
    Purpose and Role of SafariLink

    SafariLink provides technology intended to simplify the operational relationship between Operators and Suppliers.

    The Platform may provide:

    • Accommodation discovery and availability searches
    • access to authorized STO Rates
    • vehicle leasing searches and requests
    • guide discovery and availability requests
    • Transfers and activity sourcing
    • Itinerary creation
    • Trip costing and margin calculations
    • Quotation preparation
    • Reservation management
    • Supplier communication;
    • Operational notifications
    • Document storage
    • Business analytics

    Unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing, SafariLink acts as a technology platform and does not itself provide accommodation, vehicles, guides, transfers, or activities.

    Suppliers remain responsible for the Services they provide, while Operators remain responsible for their own client relationships, quotations, travel arrangements, and contractual obligations.

    2
    Eligibility and Operation Verification

    SafariLink is primarily intended for legitimate tourism businesses and authorized industry professionals.

    To register, an Operator must:

    • Provide accurate business information
    • Have the authority to represent the registered business
    • Maintain any licenses or registrations required for its activities
    • Use the Platform for legitimate business purpose
    • Comply with applicable laws
    • Accept these Operator Terms

    SafariLink may request documentation to verify an Operator, including business registration, tourism licenses, tax information, company contact details, or other reasonably necessary information.

    Approval is not a guarantee of the Operator's financial standing, professional quality, or legal compliance.

    SafariLink may decline, restrict, or suspend an account where verification requirements are not met.

    3
    Operator Account Responsibilities

    Operators must maintain accurate account information and protect their login credentials.

    You agree to:

    • Keep your account information current
    • Restrict access to authorized employees
    • Use appropriate passwords and security measures;
    • Notify SafariLink of suspected unauthorized access;
    • Assign suitable permissions to team members;
    • Remove access for employees who no longer require it
    • Accept responsibility for authorized activity performed through your account

    Operators must not share accounts in ways that compromise confidentiality, security, or accountability.

    SafariLink may introduce role-based access for owners, managers, reservations teams, finance teams, itinerary planners, and other authorized employees.

    4
    Access To Suppliers and Services

    Access To Suppliers and Services

    SafariLink enables Operators to discover and request services from participating Suppliers.

    These may include:

    Accommodation

    Hotels, lodges, camps, tented camps, villas, resorts, and other accommodation properties.

    Vehicles

    Safari Land Cruisers, safari vans, transfer vans, luxury vehicles, and other vehicles offered for lease

    Guides

    Professional safari guides, mountain guides, cultural guides, and other qualified guiding professionals.

    Transfers

    Airport transfers, intercity transfers, and other transportation services.

    Activities and Experiences

    Wildlife activities, cultural experiences, excursions, and other tourism products

    The availability of a Supplier or Service on SafariLink does not guarantee that the Supplier will accept a request.

    5
    Private STO Rates and Commercial Confidentiality

    SafariLink is designed to support B2B tourism transactions, including access to private STO Rates rather than relying exclusively on public Rack Rates.

    An Operator may access private rates only where authorized by the relevant Supplier or SafariLink.

    STO Rates may be subject to:

    • Seasonal validity
    • Minimum stay requirements
    • Room occupancy conditions
    • Vehicle lease duration requirements
    • Blackout dates
    • Payment deadlines
    • Cancellation conditions
    • Specific inclusions and exclusions
    • Other Supplier-specific conditions

    Operators must treat private STO Rates as confidential commercial information.

    You must not, without authorization:

    • Publishing private rates on public websites
    • Distributing private rate sheets to unauthorized parties
    • Sharing rates with competing businesses
    • Using automated tools to scrape or extract restricted rate information
    • Using Supplier contracts or rate sheets for unauthorized purposes
    • Misrepresenting a private net rate as a public retail rate

    Operators may use authorized rates to calculate their own client quotations and selling prices.

    Confidentiality obligations do not prevent disclosures required by law or disclosures expressly authorized by the relevant Supplier.

    6
    Rate Accuracy and Validity

    Operators are responsible for reviewing the applicable rate, currency, validity period, inclusions, exclusions, and commercial conditions before issuing a quotation or confirming a Service.

    Rates displayed on SafariLink may be supplied or updated by Suppliers.

    Where a rate is expired, incomplete, or inconsistent with the Supplier's current terms, the Operator should request clarification before relying on it.

    A rate displayed in search results does not automatically guarantee availability or constitute a confirmed reservation.

    Where a Supplier has confirmed a Reservation at an agreed rate, any subsequent change should be handled according to the applicable Reservation terms and any valid agreement between the parties.

    7
    Availability and Reservation

    Operators may use SafariLink to search for available accommodation, vehicles, guides, transfers, and other Services.

    Availability may be displayed as:

    • Available
    • On Request
    • Unavailable
    • Other status defined by the Platform

    Operators acknowledge that availability may change before a Reservation is confirmed.

    A Reservation Request is not a confirmed booking unless the Platform or an authorized Supplier communication explicitly confirms it.

    Operators must check the confirmation status before representing a Service as secured for a traveler.

    8
    Reservation Managements

    Operators are responsible for submitting complete and accurate Reservation Requests.

    Depending on the Service, the request may include:

    • Arrival and departure dates
    • Number of travelers
    • Room configuration
    • Vehicle category
    • Lease duration
    • Guide requirements
    • Pickup and drop-off locations
    • Itinerary details
    • Dietary or accessibility requirements
    • Traveler information
    • Special requests

    Operators must review the details before submitting a request.

    If additional information is required, the Operator must respond promptly to avoid delays or loss of availability.

    9
    Confirmed Managements

    Once a Reservation is confirmed, the Operator must retain the confirmation details and communicate relevant information to the appropriate parties.

    The confirmation may specify:

    • Booking reference
    • Supplier
    • Service
    • Dates
    • Quantity
    • Agreed rate
    • Inclusions and exclusions
    • Payment conditions
    • Cancellation policy
    • Special terms

    Operators should not assume that an unconfirmed request, draft itinerary, quotation, or provisional hold constitutes a confirmed booking.

    10
    Cancellations and Modifications

    Each Supplier may establish its own cancellation, modification, deposit, and refund conditions.

    Before confirming a Reservation, Operators should review the applicable conditions and communicate material restrictions to their clients.

    Changes to dates, rooms, vehicles, guides, traveler numbers, or other Reservation details are subject to availability and Supplier approval.

    Additional charges may apply.

    An Operator must not represent a requested change as confirmed until the relevant Supplier or authorized Platform workflow confirms it./p>

    11
    Operator Cancellations

    Where an Operator requests cancellation, the applicable Supplier cancellation policy and Reservation terms will determine the consequences.

    The Operator is responsible for understanding any applicable:

    • Cancellation fees
    • Non-refundable deposits
    • Modification charges
    • No-show penalties
    • Credit arrangements
    • Payment obligations

    If an Operator has entered into a separate agreement with a Supplier, that agreement may govern the cancellation process.

    SafariLink does not guarantee a refund where the applicable terms do not provide for one.

    12
    Itinerary Builder

    SafariLink may provide tools for creating, editing, costing, and managing itineraries.

    Operators are responsible for reviewing the accuracy and feasibility of each itinerary before presenting it to clients.

    This includes checking:

    • Dates and travel durations
    • Park and destination routing
    • Accommodation availability
    • Vehicle availability
    • Guide availability
    • Transfer arrangements
    • Activity schedules
    • Travel times
    • Park fees and other charges
    • Meal plans
    • Supplier inclusions and exclusions
    • Overall commercial feasibility of the trip

    An itinerary generated by the Platform or assisted by AI is not proof that its individual Services are available or confirmed.

    Operators must independently verify material details and obtain required Supplier confirmations.

    13
    Quotations, Costing, and Profit Margins

    SafariLink may provide tools to calculate trip costs, selling prices, markups, margins, commissions, taxes, and other financial information.

    Operators are responsible for checking calculations and assumptions before issuing a quotation.

    You must verify:

    • Supplier rates
    • Currency
    • Exchange rates
    • Park fees
    • Conservation fees
    • Taxes
    • Transfers
    • Guide costs
    • Vehicle lease charges
    • Accommodation costs
    • Commissions
    • Discounts
    • Contingency allowances
    • Other applicable expenses

    Operators are responsible for the prices they quote to their clients.

    SafariLink does not guarantee the profitability or commercial viability of an itinerary.
