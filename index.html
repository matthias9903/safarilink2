How It Works

From inquiry to confirmed trip — in one workflow.

SafariLink brings the key operational steps of a Tanzania trip into one connected workflow.

01

Build Your Trip

Enter your client's dates, destinations, number of travelers and requirements.

02

Source Suppliers

Find accommodation, vehicles and guides based on availability, location, rates and requirements.

03

Build Your Itinerary

Arrange the trip day by day and add your selected suppliers to the itinerary.

04

Know Your Numbers

See supplier costs, markup, selling price and estimated margin before sending your quote.

05

Request Reservations

Send and track supplier reservation requests from one operational workspace.

06

Operate the Trip

Keep your itinerary, suppliers, reservations and operational information organized in one place.
