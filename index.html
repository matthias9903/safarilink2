The Problem

Tanzania’s tourism businesses are connected in real life — but not on one system.

Tour operators, lodges, fleet owners and guides still run bookings across WhatsApp, email, phone calls and spreadsheets. That slows everyone down and leaves gaps in the trip.

Tour operators

Scattered sourcing

Hunting for rooms, cars and guides across chats and spreadsheets — then rebuilding the same quote by hand.

Accommodation

Invisible inventory

Availability and STO rates live offline, so operators cannot see what is actually bookable in real time.

Fleet partners

Charter chaos

Lease requests arrive by message; confirmations, drivers and vehicle status are hard to track in one place.

Guides

Job requests lost in chat

Assignments, parks, dates and payments are negotiated ad hoc — with little shared history for the next trip.

SafariLink is the operating network that ties them together.

One live workspace where operators source and plan trips, and partners receive, confirm and fulfil work — with shared status from first request to paid booking.

How it works

One network. Four workspaces. One live trip.

SafariLink is not only an itinerary tool. It is the B2B operating layer between tour operators and the suppliers who deliver the safari on the ground.

1

Join the network

Tour operators, accommodation, fleet and guides each get a dedicated dashboard after SafariLink approval.

2

Publish what is live

Partners list properties, rooms, vehicles, drivers, guides, rates and availability — visible to operators in real time.

3

Source & request

Operators search the network, reserve rooms, lease vehicles and request guides with clear dates and notes.

4

Confirm & quote

Partners confirm, send proformas with bank details, and mark paid — operators see status update on their side.

5

Build the client trip

Operators assemble days, costs and a client quote from confirmed services — without rebuilding everything in Excel.

6

Operate together

Messages, tasks, ratings and trip progress stay linked across dashboards until the safari is delivered.

Tour operator
