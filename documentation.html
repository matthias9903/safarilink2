# Rates & Pricing

Core pricing terminology used across the platform and supplier network.

Rack Rate The standard public or published rate where applicable.
STO Rate A private B2B commercial rate available to eligible users.
Supplier Cost The amount payable to the supplier under the applicable commercial arrangement.
Markup The amount added to the operational cost.
Gross Margin The difference between the selling price and the relevant cost.

# Availability Documentation

Availability should have clear, consistent states so operators and suppliers share the same understanding.

Available — Service appears available On Request — Supplier confirmation is required Requested — A request has been submitted Confirmed — Supplier has confirmed Unavailable — The requested service cannot be provided

# AI Documentation

SafariLink Intelligence is documented separately from core platform features.

AI can assist with

Search Planning Matching Costing Communication Translation Summaries Document extraction Operational recommendations

AI cannot independently guarantee

  • Supplier availability
  • Booking confirmation
  • Price validity
  • Payment completion
  • Service delivery
The platform's authoritative operational records remain the source of truth. AI-generated information should always be verified before material business decisions.

# Data & Privacy Documentation

Documentation should explain how information is handled across the platform.

What is storedInformation entered into accounts, profiles, itineraries and reservations.
Why it is storedTo operate the platform and connect operators with suppliers.
Who can access itRestricted by role-based permissions.
PermissionsAccess is granted by role and company settings.
RetentionStored for as long as necessary for operations and compliance.
User requestsUsers can request changes or correction through support.
Users should also review SafariLink's Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Supplier Terms and Cookie Policy.

# Documentation Philosophy

SafariLink documentation follows three principles.

Clear Users should understand what a function does without technical knowledge.
Practical Every article should help the user complete a real task.
Current Documentation should reflect the actual version and functionality of SafariLink.
