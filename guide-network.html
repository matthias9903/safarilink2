Guide Network

Match every safari with the right professional guide.

SafariLink gives operators a reliable way to source guides and gives guides a professional channel to present their experience, rates and availability.

Two sides of guide matching

How operators and guides interact

Tour operator

Matches a guide to the guests, route and type of Tanzania experience being sold.

  • Searches professional profiles by specialty, location, experience and languages.
  • Reviews certifications, ratings, vehicle arrangements, daily rate and availability.
  • Assigns the right guide to itinerary days and includes the rate in trip costing.
  • Sends a request and tracks whether the guide is confirmed for the trip.

Professional guide

Maintains a clear professional profile that helps the right operators find them.

  • Shares specialist knowledge, qualifications, languages and areas of operation.
  • Updates availability, daily rate and relevant service arrangements.
  • Receives trip requests with dates and requirements before confirming work.
  • Uses confirmed trip information to prepare for a smooth guest experience.
