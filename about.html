What We Are

A B2B operating network built for Tanzania tourism.

SafariLink is a purpose-built platform that brings together Tanzania’s fragmented tourism operations into one connected workspace. Rather than juggling spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads, phone calls, and email chains, tour operators, accommodation providers, vehicle fleet owners, and professional guides all work from the same real-time information.

From sourcing a Serengeti camp with STO rates, to assigning a Land Cruiser, booking a certified guide, building a day-by-day itinerary, and generating a client-ready quote — SafariLink keeps every step inside a single platform designed around the operational realities of the Tanzania tourism industry.

Who We Serve

Built for everyone in the supply chain.

Tour Operators

Source accommodation, vehicles, and guides. Build itineraries, calculate costs, generate quotes, and manage reservations — all in one place.

Accommodation Providers

List properties, manage room types and availability, set STO rates, receive bookings, and connect directly with tour operators.

Fleet Owners

Register safari vehicles, manage availability, upload documents, and receive booking requests from verified operators.

Professional Guides

Showcase qualifications, languages, specialties, and availability. Get matched with operators who need certified guides for their trips.

Why SafariLink

Designed in Tanzania. Built for Tanzanian tourism.

SafariLink isn't a generic travel platform adapted for the region — it was conceived and is being built around the specific operational realities of Tanzania's tourism industry. From B2B STO rates and negotiated supplier contracts to vehicle fleet coordination, guide certification tracking, and multi-currency quoting, every feature reflects how tourism businesses in Tanzania actually work.

By replacing fragmented communication channels with a single source of truth, SafariLink reduces errors, speeds up the quoting process, improves supplier relationships, and gives tour operators more time to focus on what matters: delivering exceptional experiences.
