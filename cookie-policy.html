Cookie Policy

How SafariLink Tanzania B2B uses cookies and similar technologies across its website and Platform.

Effective Date: [Insert Date] Last Updated: [Insert Date]
This Cookie Policy explains how SafariLink Tanzania B2B (“SafariLink”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit our website or use the SafariLink Platform.

This Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

1 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website.

They allow websites to remember information about your visit, maintain sessions, improve security, understand how the website is used, and provide a better user experience.

SafariLink may also use similar technologies such as:

  • session storage;
  • local storage;
  • pixels;
  • web beacons;
  • device identifiers; and
  • other technologies that perform similar functions.

2 Why SafariLink Uses Cookies

SafariLink uses cookies and similar technologies to:

  • keep users securely logged in;
  • remember user preferences;
  • maintain Platform sessions;
  • protect Accounts;
  • prevent fraudulent activity;
  • understand how visitors use the website;
  • improve Platform performance;
  • measure product usage;
  • troubleshoot technical problems;
  • improve search and navigation;
  • remember selected settings;
  • support communication functionality; and
  • improve our services and products.

3 Types of Cookies We May Use

SafariLink may use the following categories of cookies.

3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are required for the website or Platform to function properly. They may be used for:

  • authentication;
  • account sessions;
  • security;
  • fraud prevention;
  • load balancing;
  • navigation;
  • maintaining user preferences required for Platform functionality; and
  • other essential technical functions.

These cookies generally cannot be disabled through our cookie preference tool because the Platform may not function properly without them.

4 Functional Cookies

Functional cookies allow SafariLink to remember choices you make. For example, they may remember:

  • language preferences;
  • currency preferences;
  • dashboard settings;
  • search preferences;
  • display preferences;
  • recently selected filters; and
  • other personalization settings.

These cookies help provide a smoother experience without requiring you to repeatedly enter the same information.

5 Analytics and Performance Cookies

Where enabled and permitted, SafariLink may use analytics technologies to understand how the website and Platform are used. These technologies may help us understand:

  • which pages are visited;
  • which Platform features are used;
  • how users navigate the Platform;
  • how long users spend on particular areas;
  • technical errors;
  • device and browser performance;
  • general traffic patterns; and
  • how we can improve the Platform.

Analytics information may be aggregated or otherwise processed to understand Platform performance.

6 Security Cookies

Security-related cookies and similar technologies may be used to:

  • authenticate users;
  • detect suspicious activity;
  • prevent unauthorized access;
  • protect accounts;
  • identify abnormal sessions;
  • prevent fraud; and
  • protect the Platform and its users.

These technologies may operate automatically as part of our security systems.

7 Marketing Cookies

SafariLink may use marketing or advertising technologies in the future to understand the effectiveness of marketing campaigns or display relevant business-related communications.

Where required by applicable law, we will obtain consent before using non-essential marketing cookies.

You may be able to reject or withdraw consent for these cookies through our cookie preference controls.

8 Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies or similar technologies may be placed or operated by third-party service providers that help us provide our Platform. These may include providers supporting:

  • analytics;
  • cloud infrastructure;
  • authentication;
  • payment processing;
  • maps;
  • communication;
  • cybersecurity;
  • customer support;
  • marketing; and
  • other Platform services.

Third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and contractual arrangements with SafariLink.

SafariLink does not control every cookie or technology operated by an independent third party.

9 Cookies Used by the B2B Platform

When you log into the SafariLink B2B Platform, cookies or similar technologies may be required to maintain your secure session. For example, they may help the Platform remember:

  • that you are authenticated;
  • your authorized account;
  • your selected company;
  • your role and permissions;
  • your dashboard preferences;
  • selected search filters; and
  • other temporary session information.

These technologies are important for maintaining secure access to business information.

10 Private STO Rates and Cookies

SafariLink may use session and authentication technologies to ensure that private commercial information, including STO Rates, is shown only to appropriately authorized users.

Cookies themselves do not determine commercial eligibility.

Access to private STO Rates is controlled through the Platform's account, authorization, and permission systems.

11 AI Features

SafariLink may introduce AI-powered features such as:

  • intelligent search;
  • itinerary assistance;
  • supplier matching;
  • quote assistance;
  • operational recommendations; and
  • business intelligence.

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to remember relevant user preferences or maintain the technical session required to provide these features.

AI processing and personal-data processing are governed separately by our Privacy Policy and applicable agreements.

12 Cookie Consent

Where required by applicable law, SafariLink will ask for your consent before placing non-essential cookies on your device. You may be presented with options such as:

  • Accept All — Allows all categories of cookies that are available through the consent mechanism.
  • Reject Non-Essential — Allows only cookies necessary for the website or Platform to function.
  • Cookie Settings — Allows you to choose individual categories.

Your choices may be stored so that you do not have to provide them repeatedly.

13 Withdrawing or Changing Your Consent

Where applicable, you may change your cookie preferences at any time through SafariLink's cookie settings.

Changing your preferences does not necessarily delete cookies that have already been placed on your device.

You may also delete cookies through your browser settings.

14 Browser Controls

Most modern browsers allow you to:

  • view cookies;
  • delete cookies;
  • block cookies;
  • allow cookies;
  • block third-party cookies; or
  • receive warnings before cookies are stored.

However, disabling necessary cookies may cause some SafariLink features to stop functioning correctly.

15 Do-Not-Track Signals

Some browsers provide "Do Not Track" or similar privacy signals.

SafariLink will respond to such signals where required by applicable law or where technically appropriate.

16 Information Collected Through Cookies

Depending on the type of technology used, information collected may include:

  • IP address;
  • browser type;
  • device type;
  • operating system;
  • session identifiers;
  • pages visited;
  • Platform features used;
  • approximate location;
  • referral information;
  • timestamps;
  • language preferences;
  • security information; and
  • other technical or usage information.

SafariLink will process information in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable law.

17 Cookies and Personal Information

Some cookies may be associated with information that can identify or relate to an individual.

Where cookie information constitutes personal data, SafariLink will process it in accordance with applicable data-protection requirements.

Our Privacy Policy explains more about:

  • what personal information we collect;
  • why we process it;
  • how we share it;
  • how we protect it; and
  • your applicable data-protection rights.

18 Cookie Retention

Session Cookies

These generally expire when you close your browser or when the relevant session ends.

Persistent Cookies

These remain on your device for a defined period or until they are deleted.

The retention period depends on the purpose of the cookie and the technology provider.

SafariLink aims to retain cookies only for as long as reasonably necessary for their intended purpose.

  • changes in third-party providers;

Where required, we may provide additional notice or request renewed consent.
