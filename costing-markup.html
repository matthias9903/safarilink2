Costing & Markups

Make every trip price clear, defensible and profitable.

SafariLink connects trade rates and confirmed service costs to the operator’s quote, while keeping each supplier’s commercial information controlled.

Two sides of commercial clarity

How operators use supplier rates responsibly

Tour operator

Builds a commercial view of the trip after choosing the services needed to operate it.

  • Adds accommodation, vehicle, guide and activity items with quantity and unit cost.
  • Applies the selected supplier rate and calculates total cost automatically.
  • Sets a margin to see markup, recommended sell price and estimated profit.
  • Uses a quote preview before moving into reservation confirmation.

Supplier or service provider

Provides commercial information that helps the operator price accurately.

  • Maintains B2B / STO rate, daily rate or service price for approved trade use.
  • Clarifies rate conditions, availability and any applicable requirements.
  • Responds if the requested date, rate or service has changed.
  • Benefits when quotes reflect the correct service and realistic operational cost.
