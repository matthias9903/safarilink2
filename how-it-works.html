How It Works

From client inquiry to confirmed Tanzania trip.

SafariLink connects the entire operational workflow in one place — sourcing, planning, costing, quoting, reserving, operating and closing each trip through a single linked B2B platform.

One workflow, three phases. Plan the trip (Steps 1–5), price and sell it (Steps 6–9), then operate and close it (Steps 10–11) — everything tracked in one connected workspace.
