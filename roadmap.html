Roadmap

Our Roadmap

Building the infrastructure behind Tanzania tourism.

SafariLink is being built around one simple idea: tourism businesses should spend less time coordinating suppliers and more time selling and delivering great Tanzania experiences.

Our roadmap focuses on making the platform progressively more useful, connected and intelligent while maintaining the reliability required for real tourism operations. We are developing SafariLink in stages, starting with the operational foundation and gradually adding deeper automation, intelligence, integrations and network capabilities.

Phase 1

The Foundation

Current / Foundation

Build the core B2B operating network. The first stage focuses on the essential infrastructure required to connect tourism businesses.

Core capabilities

  • Tour operator accounts
  • Supplier accounts
  • Business verification
  • Accommodation profiles
  • Room inventory
  • B2B / STO rates
  • Vehicle fleet profiles
  • Vehicle availability
  • Professional guide profiles
  • Guide availability
  • Activity and experience listings
  • Supplier and global search
  • Supplier messaging
  • Availability requests
  • Reservation management
  • Itinerary builder
  • Cost calculation
  • Markups and margins
  • Client quotes
  • Documents and notifications
  • User roles and permissions
GoalCreate one reliable place where Tanzania tourism businesses can find, compare, plan and manage the services required to operate trips.
Phase 2

Connected Operations

Connect the entire trip workflow. The second stage connects individual platform features into a continuous operational workflow.

Inquiry → Search → Availability → Cost → Quote → Reservation → Confirmation → Operation

Planned capabilities

  • Central reservation calendar
  • Supplier confirmation tracking
  • Automated reminders and deadlines
  • Booking reference management
  • Client and trip task management
  • Supplier response tracking
  • Reservation documents and vouchers
  • Booking summaries
  • Invoice management
  • Payment tracking
  • Operational dashboards
  • Team assignments and internal notes
  • Trip status monitoring
GoalMove from simply finding suppliers to actually running trips through SafariLink.
Phase 3

Intelligent SafariLink

Introduce intelligence into the operating workflow. SafariLink Intelligence will help tourism professionals make faster and better operational decisions.

Planned capabilities

  • Natural-language search
  • AI itinerary assistant
  • AI supplier, guide and vehicle matching
  • AI costing assistant
  • AI quote drafting
  • AI reservation follow-up
  • AI communication assistant
  • Automatic document extraction
  • Rate-sheet and supplier information extraction
  • Translation assistance
  • Operational and margin alerts
  • Trip risk detection
  • Alternative supplier recommendations
Example“Find a 4-star lodge near Serengeti for 2 adults, September 15–17, with breakfast and an available STO rate.” SafariLink searches its actual supplier inventory and returns relevant options.
Important principleSafariLink Intelligence works from verified platform data. It should never invent hotels, prices, availability, supplier confirmations, vehicle or guide availability, or reservation status. Critical booking and financial actions remain subject to user confirmation.
Phase 4

Network Expansion

Expand the Tanzania tourism supply network once the operating foundation is established.

Potential categories

  • More accommodation providers
  • Vehicle fleets and transfer companies
  • Guides and activities
  • Cultural experiences
  • Zanzibar suppliers
  • Kilimanjaro operators
  • Aviation partners
  • Boat operators and marine excursions
  • Event and conference suppliers
  • Specialised transport
  • Luxury services
GoalMake SafariLink increasingly useful as the digital infrastructure connecting Tanzania's tourism supply chain.
Phase 5

Connect SafariLink with the systems tourism businesses already use. Integrations will be introduced based on user demand, technical feasibility and operational value.

Potential integrations

  • Email and WhatsApp
  • Calendar systems
  • CRM platforms
  • Booking systems
  • Property management systems
  • Channel managers

  • Document storage
  • Business intelligence tools
Phase 6

Tanzania Tourism Intelligence

Turn operational data into business intelligence. As the network grows, SafariLink can help businesses understand the patterns that make better decisions possible.

Insights may include

  • Demand and destination patterns
  • Supplier performance
  • Average booking value
  • Trip profitability
  • Seasonal trends
  • Reservation conversion
  • Cancellation patterns
  • Supplier response times
  • Fleet utilisation
  • Guide demand
  • Accommodation demand
  • Popular itinerary combinations
GoalNot simply to store information, but to help tourism businesses make better decisions.
Guiding principles

Roadmap Principles

  • Reliability over unnecessary complexity.
  • Operational value over features for the sake of features.
  • Security over convenience.
  • Real tourism workflows over generic SaaS assumptions.
  • Tanzania-first development over trying to serve every market immediately.
