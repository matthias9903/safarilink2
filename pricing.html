Pricing FAQ

Common questions, answered

Can I try SafariLink before paying?

SafariLink may offer a free trial, pilot programme or early-access arrangement to selected tourism businesses. Availability and eligibility may vary — contact the SafariLink team to discuss access.

Can I change plans?

Yes. Businesses can upgrade or downgrade according to the applicable plan terms.

Do suppliers and operators pay the same price?

Not necessarily. SafariLink uses different commercial models depending on the role and services used.

Are STO rates included?

STO rates are supplier-controlled commercial rates available only to eligible verified B2B users.

Is there an enterprise plan?

Yes. Larger tourism companies can request a custom plan.

Can I cancel?

Subscription cancellation is subject to the applicable plan terms.
