B2B Accommodation

Give operators the property information they need to plan with confidence.

SafariLink connects accommodation partners with tour operators through professional profiles, trade information, availability requests and reservations.

Two sides of the stay

How operators and properties interact

Tour operator

Uses the accommodation directory to find the right stay for the route, guest profile and budget.

  • Searches property profiles, locations, room types and operational details.
  • Reviews B2B / STO rates and conditions provided for trade partners.
  • Sends an availability request with dates, rooms and guest requirements.
  • Adds the selected property to the itinerary, costing and reservation plan.

Accommodation owner

Controls how the property is presented and responds to real business opportunities.

  • Maintains property details, room inventory, availability, policies and B2B rates.
  • Receives clear requests with travel dates and room requirements.
  • Confirms, declines or suggests suitable alternatives for an operator.
  • Tracks reservations and shares the information needed for smooth arrival.
