Vehicle Network

Connect the right fleet with the right trip.

SafariLink creates a professional operating link between tour operators planning journeys and fleet providers managing vehicles, rates and availability.

Two sides of a vehicle booking

How operators and fleet providers interact

Tour operator

Sources transport based on itinerary, route, group size and travel dates.

  • Searches vehicle type, location, seats, daily rate and fleet information.
  • Checks whether the vehicle is available and operationally suitable.
  • Adds the chosen vehicle to the itinerary and trip cost breakdown.
  • Sends a lease or booking request and monitors its confirmation status.

Fleet provider

Uses a professional profile to manage fleet information and commercial readiness.

  • Lists vehicles with type, registration, seats, location, daily rate and status.
  • Updates availability and highlights maintenance or service requirements.
  • Receives requests linked to dates, trip needs and the operator.
  • Confirms bookings and maintains the information needed for operations.
